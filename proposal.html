The idea

Your hospitality, in their pocket

When guests arrive in a new place, the first questions are always the same: where do we go, what’s worth our time, and how do we get back? Highland Compass answers all three with a small web app, branded as yours, that opens instantly on any phone — no app store, no account, no fuss.

It carries your personal recommendations, maps the best of the surrounding area, and keeps a gentle compass pointing home to the property. Everything works offline once loaded, which matters in the glens where signal disappears.

The guest experience

What it offers

Find your way home

A live compass and distance back to the property — reassurance on every walk, drive or hike.

Your recommendations

The restaurants, viewpoints and walks you’d send a friend to — curated by you, not an algorithm.

The area, mapped

Castles, distilleries, beaches and landmarks on a clean map, with directions and Street View links.

Offline & private

Works without signal once opened. No tracking, no data collected — everything stays on the guest’s phone.

Why it works for you

The value to Daviot Guest House

  • A warmer welcome. Guests feel looked after from the moment they scan a small card in the room.
  • Fewer repeat questions. The directions, opening times and “what’s nearby” answers live in one place.
  • A point of difference. A thoughtful digital touch that few small properties offer — memorable, and easy to mention in reviews.
  • Effortless and compliant. No app to install, nothing to maintain on your side, and privacy-friendly by design.
